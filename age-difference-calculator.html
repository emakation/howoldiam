Enter the dates of birth for both people. You can optionally add names to make the result more personal. Click Calculate age difference and the tool instantly shows the exact age gap in years, months, and days, along with which person is older.

This tool is commonly used by couples wanting to know their age gap, siblings comparing birth years, friends settling a debate about who is older, or anyone curious about the age difference between two people in history or popular culture.

All calculations run in your browser. No personal data is collected or stored.
