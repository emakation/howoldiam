How to use this age calculator

Simply enter your date of birth in the first field. The second field defaults to today's date, but you can change it to calculate your age on any specific date. Click Calculate my age and the tool instantly shows your exact age broken down into years, months, days, hours, and minutes.

The calculator also shows the total number of days you have been alive and counts down the days remaining until your next birthday — useful for birthday planning, passport applications, insurance forms, or satisfying everyday curiosity.

All calculations happen instantly in your browser. No data is stored or sent anywhere.
