How to use the "How old was I?" calculator

Enter your date of birth in the field above. A grid of years will appear — from the year you were born through to several years in the future. Click any year and the calculator instantly tells you how old you were, or will be, during that year.

This is useful for recalling your age during important moments — how old you were when a favourite film came out, when a family event happened, or when a historical event occurred. It also helps with future planning — how old you will be when you retire, when a child graduates, or when a milestone anniversary arrives.
